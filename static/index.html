 UNDER THE SURFACE

A signature, not a guess.

Each uploaded recording is decoded in full, converted to mono at 22,050 Hz, and normalized for overall gain. We extract 87 static measurements and three 64-bin trajectories.

The comparison

Features use median-centred, robust scaling with a standard-deviation fallback. Each group uses root-mean-square distance so texture doesn’t win just because it has more numbers. We average distances to all your reference songs and sum the five weighted groups.

D = Σ weight × group distance
similarity = 100 × exp(−D)

Structure uses constrained dynamic time warping of robust-scaled, 64-bin loudness, brightness and onset trajectories. The map uses weighted PCA of static features; nearby points can still differ in structure.

What this can tell you

Scores measure resemblance within your current collection. They are not probabilities, proof of preference, or measurements of emotion. Tempo estimates can have half/double-time ambiguity. Vocal presence and reverb are not directly classified. Small libraries give unstable scaling and maps.

The example measures original synthetic signals. The editorial band shortlist uses published genre tags and is not an acoustic result.
